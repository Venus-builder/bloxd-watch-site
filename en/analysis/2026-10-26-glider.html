🪂 Analysis report: the new glider enchantments

The effects of the three glider enchantments added in the 2026-10-26 update (build cnty9), turned into numbers from the formulas in the game program.

✅ Conclusion (the numbers you most want)

Firecracker cooldown: about 15% shorter per level (multiplicatively). Level 1 is −15.0%, level 2 −27.8%, level 3 −38.6%.
Firecracker boost: +15% stronger per level. Level 1 is +15%, level 2 +30%, level 3 +45%.
Gliding speed: +2 to top speed per level. +2 at level 1, +4 at level 2, +6 at level 3. As a percentage, at level 3 that is about +46% for a wood glider (base 13) and +24% for diamond (base 25).

Formula source: in the client (main.js), getFirecrackerCooldownMultiplier = 0.85 to the power of the level (lower limit 0.4), getFirecrackerBoostMultiplier = 1 + 0.15 × level (upper limit 2), getGlidingSpeedBonus = 2 × level (upper limit 10). The base cooldown is the setting gliderFuelCooldown: 1000 (milliseconds), and the base boost force is 50, to which the multiplier is applied.

🔎 A bit more detail

Cooldown (how much shorter)

At level L the original time is multiplied by 0.85, L times (a 15% reduction each time, multiplicatively). It is not additive, so each extra level reduces less than the one before.

In terms of how often you can use it, against the base of "once per second", level 1 is about 1.2 times, level 2 about 1.4 times and level 3 about 1.6 times (per second).

Share of the original time per level: 1: 85.0% / 2: 72.2% / 3: 61.4% / 4: 52.2% / 5: 44.4% / 6: 40.0% / 7: 40.0% (the floor is 40%; it flattens out from level 6).

Boost (how much stronger)

At level L the push force becomes (1 + 0.15 × L) times. It is additive, so each level adds the same amount (+15%). The upper limit is 2 times (+100%), reached at level 7.

Multiplier per level: 1: 115.0% / 2: 130.0% / 3: 145.0% / 4: 160.0% / 5: 175.0% / 6: 190.0% / 7: 200.0% / 8: 200.0%

Gliding speed (top-speed bonus)

At level L the bonus is 2 × L (upper limit 10; it flattens out at level 5). According to the game documents (the Studio description), the base top speeds of gliders are wood 13, iron 17, gold 21 and diamond 25. If the bonus is simply added to this top speed, you get the following.

🎲 How the enchantments get applied

  • The target is the glider (wood, iron, gold and diamond hang gliders). Only these three can be applied.
  • The higher the enchantment tier (Tier 1 to 5), the more times "pick one at random and raise its level by 1" happens: once for Tier 1, five times for Tier 5.
  • When applied at random, one kind reaches at most level 3 (a kind that reaches 3 is not chosen again).
  • For example, on a Tier 5 glider, a total of 5 levels are split among the three kinds. Up to 3 levels in one kind and the remaining 2 in another is the combination that maximises one effect.
  • The limits in the program (cooldown level 6, boost level 7, speed level 5) look like safety nets for when a high level is applied by a command or some special means. In normal play you only reach level 3.

Source: the code that decides enchantments (rolls per tier {"Tier 1":1 … "Tier 5":5}, cap per kind 3) and the glider enchantment list Glider: ["Firecracker Cooldown","Firecracker Boost","Gliding Speed"].

🚀 How firecrackers are used (related behaviour)

  • The boost works while gliding with the hang glider in hand and a firecracker held. Each use consumes one firecracker (not in creative mode).
  • The boost goes in the direction you are looking (mostly horizontal). The momentum fades back to normal gliding over a fixed time (about 100 ticks).
  • You cannot use a firecracker during the cooldown. A bar appears near the middle of the screen.

🧪 Other analysis of this update

  • The formulas of the other enchantments did not change. I compared the existing formulas for bows, pickaxes, axes, armor and so on (Quick Charge, arrow speed, damage calculation and others) between the old and new builds, and the numbers were the same. The only formulas added in this update are the three glider ones. (Only these formulas were checked, not every item stat.)
  • The Build a Factory 2 "Islands" screen code (the "Islands" button inside the core) is in the program. The number of islands you can have uses a value sent from the server (baf2IslandsMax), so it cannot be known from the client code.
  • The "Standing Noticeboard" remains in the program as a block. Only its display-name entry disappeared from the dictionary (translations).
  • The animal sound data disappeared from the main file (main.js), but it remains in the list of sound names (the Studio material). It is most likely that only the place where the data is stored moved.
  • The official announcements (the in-game news list) did not yet include an item for this update. The newest item was "Skyblock Official Trailer" on October 21.

⚠ Limits of this report

  • Nothing here was measured by playing the game. The numbers are computed from the formulas and settings of the game client (the program served to the browser).
  • The server may judge with a different formula from the client (cooldown and boost in particular should also be checked on the server, but that code cannot be seen).
  • How to obtain them (which tier of enchantment, and how they appear) is server-side information and unknown.
  • This is a new feature, so the numbers may change in later updates. Update Watch will detect a change.
