Players

"Lucky Skin Block" (a free cosmetic drop)

A free block you can open, with "Next in N h", so it seems to be once per interval. It shows how many pieces you have collected and the odds for each piece you do not have yet. Drops: hair, eyebrows, eyes, top, trousers, shoes, emotes. After opening you compare Before / After and choose whether to equip. It can offer reminders (at most one a day, never at night; can be turned off in Settings), and Settings also has "Hide Lucky Skin Block". Locked parts say "From the Lucky Skin Block". From the public code: whether it shows depends on a state returned by the server. If the next-ready time is in the future it says "Next in N h" (rounded up); if it has passed and it can be claimed, it offers to open. It is hidden when the hide setting is on, when everything is collected, or when the account is not in the drop (empty state). The dot on the Store button appears for that, and the pointer bubble only on the home screen when it can be opened and the browser has played at least once. The reminder prompt appears only if reminders are off, you are outside a game, and you were not asked before.

Who sees it, how often it can be opened, and the hours are decided by the server and were not read. There is no time-of-day window in the client; it seems to be openable any time after the next-ready time. The interval and the odds are placeholders ({{count}}, {{percent}}). The push reminders (once a day at most, never at night) were not read on the sending side.

Confidence: Text and on-screen conditions are certain (public code read) / who and when are server-side and unchecked
