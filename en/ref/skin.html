Skin viewer (a tool for checking how players look)

What a home-made look-checking tool does, and how player looks work.

This page summarises the features of a home-made "Bloxd Skin Viewer" and what it showed about how skins and cosmetics work. The viewer embeds the official player model and official textures. The findings below come from the viewer's research notes and I have not re-checked them against the original build (marked From the viewer notes). Build checked: fsa8nn (not the date of today's build).

1. What the viewer can do

TabWhat it does
Held itemsPut an item in the right or left hand. Rotation, position and size are sliders (the left hand uses a mirrored placement; values are saved per hand).
PoseNine presets (stand, wave, victory, point, think, salute, shrug, relax, aim) and free rotation of every joint of head, both arms, both legs and torso with sliders (X = forward/back, Y = twist, Z = sideways, in degrees). Drag to turn the view, wheel to zoom.
LookChoose one of the 8 default characters (Characters, Default) in the game. Armor (pick material and slot, full set, remove all). Paste a cosmetic string (JSON or a name like skin_0_5) and apply.
MobsShow the mobs in the official model with a simple walk of legs and arms (player tabs are unavailable meanwhile).
OutputSave a transparent PNG, or a "64×64-style skin image" with the cells of the current look laid out on one sheet.

2. Findings (how the looks work) From the viewer notes

TopicContent
Player modelFile Player_Model.glb: 32 meshes, Draco-compressed. The head is 9×9×9 texels, arms and legs have three segments. Eyes and eyebrows are separate flat meshes stuck in front of the face.
TextureFile Player_Atlas.png (2048×2048) holding 660 cells of 64 px. It is inside the served files as a base64 string, and the game uses the same file (getTexture("Player_Atlas")).
Look settingsDecided by "cosmetic names" for skin / head / eyebrows / eyes / body / legs / shoes / back / hat (e.g. body_4_3). Each layer's cell origin is added to the UV, the whole mesh is drawn once per layer and cut out by transparency. Order: skin, head, eyebrows, eyes, body, legs, shoes.
ArmorThe armor meshes are inside the official model. The texture is Player_Armour.png (4096×64); materials are in the official order and the UV is u=(uv.x+material index)/64. Face openings are cut out by transparency.
PosesNodes such as ArmLeftMesh are rotated about the joint axis. The official API api.setPlayerPose works the same way. Blinking and walking are separate: the engine moves UVs or vertices.
Name-to-skin APIget-player-profile needs authentication and returns 401 without login (the viewer's measurement). So looking up another player's skin by name was not added.
