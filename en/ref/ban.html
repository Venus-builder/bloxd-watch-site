Ban investigation (the reported wave of banned accounts)

What is checked and what is not. The cause has not been identified.

This page summarises, as far as can be published, the reported ban of many accounts around 2026-11-19, separating "checked" from "not checked". The cause has not been identified. Ban decisions are made on the server and cannot be seen from the client code.

1. What was reported (by the user, unchecked)

  • All accounts, main and alt, were banned. Several acquaintances appear to be in the same situation.
  • There is a claim that the team's own accounts were banned too (hearsay; I have not verified it).
  • The time of the ban, the exact message shown and the network used have not been collected yet.

3. Relation to the recent updates

  • In the text differences of the three updates from 11-18 to 11-19, nothing reads as a change to how bans work. Certain
  • 11-19 redid the report categories (removed "Suggestive"; added child safety, real-life threats, self-harm and sexual messages). How reports are handled afterwards may have changed, but the link to bans is unknown. Unknown
  • 11-19 added measurements for switching the route to game servers through Cloudflare. That looks unrelated to bans. Guess

5. Information that would narrow it down

  • When you noticed the ban (to the minute) and what you were doing just before
  • The message shown and the error number (4020 / 4021 / 4023 and so on)
  • The network (home, school, VPN, mobile) and whether others share it
  • Whether several accounts were used in the same browser
  • Whether any extensions or scripts were used
  • Whether the number of banned people keeps growing over time

If a common factor shows up, it can be narrowed to chaining, reports or a threshold change.

6. What this site does not do

  • It does not join lobbies or connect to servers for investigation, because that risks spreading bans.
  • It does not look for ways to avoid bans or for holes in the detection.
  • It does not run or publish the active tests suggested in the report (the kind that attack another party's service without permission).

This is an unofficial investigation, not connected to the bloxd.io team. It is based only on reading the publicly served client files and observing traffic. Not translated yet: the "Hidden games" page, the guides and the seed map (Japanese only).
