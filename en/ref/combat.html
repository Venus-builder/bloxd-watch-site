Unknown (the client has no numbers): the size of Attack Speed, Health, Health Regen, Knockback Resist, the various Auras, the multiplier of Critical Damage for melee weapons, how much each Protection level adds, and attack intervals other than the dagger's. The descriptions say Attack Speed = "enemies you hit take damage more often" and Critical Damage = "damage when you hit while falling".

Damage taken Certain (from the gun damage code)

Damage taken = original damage × (1 − total armor) × adjustment × damage-taken multiplier
  • Adjustment: 0.4B/3 when B>0, 2 − 0.4|B|/3 when B<0, and 1 when B=0
  • B = total Protection (a value the server provides) + (Damage Reduction level)0.8 − (Weakness level)0.65

Worked examples Certain (formulas applied)

A diamond sword (base damage 40) with Damage Lv 1 to 3 gives about 48.7 / 53.2 / 56.3 (+21.8% / +33.1% / +40.8%). A diamond dagger (4 hits per second) deals roughly 144 / 177 / 194 / 206 per second at Damage Lv 0 to 3.

The combination that maximises a sword's damage per second cannot be computed because the size of Attack Speed is unknown. Server-side values are best measured in your own world, which has not been done yet.
