🏆 Handy info: best enchantment combinations

The effect (%) of every enchantment and the "strongest split" for each tool, computed from the formulas in the game program (build fo1voe).

⚠️ Please read this first

Only enchantments whose formula is written in the game client (the program that runs on your machine) are turned into numbers. The size of Attack Speed, Protection (per level), Health, Health Regen, Knockback Resist, the various Auras, and the Critical Damage multiplier for melee weapons seem to have their numbers on the server, and I could not read them from the code even after re-checking. They are marked "unknown" and no made-up numbers are given. For that reason "the combination that maximises a sword's damage per second" cannot be given with certainty (see "Swords" below).
Correction: the first version said "the bow's base damage is not in the code" and used 10 and 40 as stand-ins. A re-check found the base damage in the bow definition (wood 37 to diamond 68), so the bow section was rebuilt with the real values. Weapon range, knockback and dash numbers were added too.

🛡️ Armor

Armor defence (armourReduction) is in the code per piece. The totals of the five pieces are wood 0.41, iron 0.56, gold 0.67 and diamond 0.77 (diamond: helmet 0.13, chest 0.24, leggings 0.19, boots 0.10, gauntlets 0.11). No definition was found for stone armor. The fur chestplate is 0.22 (knockback reduction 1) and the spiked boots are 0.09.
Correction: the first version left out the gauntlets and gave a total of 0.66.

Damage taken is decided by the formula in the code like this (a formula confirmed in the gun damage code):

Damage taken = original damage × (1 − total armor) × 0.4B÷3 × damage-taken multiplier
B = total Protection (a value the server provides) + (Damage Reduction level)0.8 − (Weakness level)0.65
When B is negative, the factor is 2 − 0.4|B|÷3 instead of 0.4B÷3 (damage taken goes up).

Example: with a full diamond set (0.77) alone, damage taken is 23%. If B = 3 is added, it is multiplied by 0.4 again, to about 9.2%.

However, the "effect per level" of Protection, Health and Health Regen (how much B each Protection level adds) is not in the code, so the best split cannot be given.

🧭 Summary

  • A higher tier means more rolls, so it is easier to raise the enchantment you want. But which enchantments you get is luck; you cannot aim for them.
  • For tiers where "chance of getting it" is low, the "Random average" column is closer to what you will really get.
  • Every number here is a computation of a "formula in the game program". Nothing was confirmed by playing, so please tell me if you find a discrepancy.
