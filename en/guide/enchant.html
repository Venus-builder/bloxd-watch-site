✨ Handy info: enchantment quick table

The enchantment system and the effect numbers, put together from the formulas written in the game program (build fqwnlq). This looks at what the game contains now, separately from updates.

✅ The short version

The enchanting table has five levels: wood, stone, iron, gold and diamond. A better table offers higher levels and makes higher tiers likelier.
Tier (1 to 5) is "how many times the effects are rolled". For Tier N, one of the enchantments the tool can have is chosen N times, each time raising its level by +1.
The level of one enchantment is at most 3 (once it reaches 3 it is not chosen again). For example, if Tier 5 is put on a tool with three candidates, the five rolls split among the three, and the most you get is a shape like 3-2-0, 3-1-1 or 2-2-1.

🛡️ How defence works (Protection and so on)

The formula for taking damage (nM) works like this. First the "defence total B" is made by adding the armor defence enchantments and the damage-reduction effect and subtracting the weakness effect. Then damage taken becomes 0.4 to the power of (B÷3) times (the bigger B is, the smaller it gets).

B (defence total)Damage taken

❓ Things not yet turned into numbers

  • Damage / Attack Speed / Critical Damage (melee weapons): candidate formulas are in the code, but I have not confirmed which values they apply to. For gun headshots, Critical Damage is used as +8% damage per level (certain), but whether melee weapons use the same is unknown.
  • Health / Health Regen / Knockback Resist and the various Aura effects: I have not found their formulas.
  • A per-tier strength multiplier is in the code (about 1.272 times for each tier up; about 2.62 times at Tier 5), but I have not confirmed what it is used for.
  • Server-side-only values and fine adjustments other than damage cannot be seen from here.

This is an unofficial summary made by reading the game client program (build fqwnlq). I have not confirmed it by playing. If the game updates and the formulas change, it will be rebuilt.
